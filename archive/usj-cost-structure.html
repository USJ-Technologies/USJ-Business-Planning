⚠ Two things the ₹3,130 estimate missed

  • ₹550 and ₹900 are first-year promo prices. From year 2 the same domains cost ₹899 and ₹1,499 — almost triple.
  • The two domains we already own also renew every year. chalokumbh.com and usjtechnologies.com are ₹1,769 each, every year. They were not in the estimate at all.

✓ How to actually save money here

Register all four for 3–5 years at once. You lock today's price, you cannot forget to renew, and search engines treat a long registration as a sign of a serious business. Turn on auto-renew and domain privacy — losing a live domain to a missed payment is the most expensive mistake possible here.

⚠ The catch in the free plan

Zoho's Forever Free plan no longer includes IMAP, POP or ActiveSync. That means no Outlook, and no proper mail app on the phone — you can only read mail in a web browser at mail.zoho.com. For a business that answers customer enquiries from a phone, that is a real problem.

✓ Plain recommendation — Mail Lite, 5 mailboxes

₹4,177 per year. That is ₹348 a month for the entire team. Mail Lite gives phone and Outlook access, proper support, and room to add people. The free plan saves ₹4,177 a year and costs us the ability to reply to a customer from a phone. Not a good trade. Workplace Standard is worth revisiting later if we start needing shared documents and chat.

Part 5

What to do, in order

  1. Buy usjgroup.in and usjelectronics.com — ₹1,711 today. Register for 3 years if the budget allows; the price only goes up.
  2. Turn on auto-renew for all four domains. Add a calendar reminder 30 days before each renewal date as a backup.
  3. Set up Zoho Mail on usjgroup.in. Start free while testing, upgrade to Mail Lite before the mailboxes go on any visiting card, website or advertisement.
  4. Create the three business mailboxes and hand each one to the person responsible for that business.
  5. Decide on bhanu@ and jitesh@. Either keep them on usjtechnologies.com or move both to usjgroup.in so everything sits under one roof. Moving is easier now than later.
  6. Review this page every year in the renewal month. Prices change; the structure should not.
