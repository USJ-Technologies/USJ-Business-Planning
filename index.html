ChaloKumbh · Service one

Online Pooja — live

A devotee books a pooja on our website. Our pandit performs it at the ghat in Haridwar. The devotee watches it live, as it happens.

Why we can do this. Every other platform sends a recorded video afterwards. Even the big apps. Nobody shows the pooja live.

We are on the ghat ourselves — not booking through a middleman.

Online Pooja

How it works

  1. Devotee opens the website and chooses a pooja
  2. They enter their name and gotra, and pick a date and time
  3. They pay online
  4. We send them a private link for their pooja
  5. At the fixed time, our person sets up the camera at the ghat
  6. The pandit performs the pooja, taking their name and gotra
  7. The devotee watches it live from anywhere in the world
  8. After it finishes, we send them the recording too
  9. We courier the prasad and Ganga Jal to their home

Online Pooja

How we get customers

The main idea: give the aarti away free, sell the pooja.

Stream Ganga Aarti live from Har Ki Pauri every evening, free, on YouTube and Instagram. The people who watch Ganga Aarti online are exactly the people who book a Ganga pooja. Costs nothing extra — the camera and the person are already there.

  • NRI ads — Indian origin, Hindu, living in USA, UK, Canada, UAE, Australia, Singapore
  • Occasion timing — Shivratri, Navratri, Pitra Paksha, Shraddh, and the Kumbh snan dates
  • Search — people already look for "online pooja Haridwar" and "Ganga pooja booking"
  • Word of mouth — one shraddh pooja and the whole family hears about it
  • The pandit's own network of yajmans
ChaloKumbh · Service two

Vedanz

Two products. Same name, different work.

Ganga Jal
Delivered anywhere

Authentic Haridwar Ganga Jal, filled and sealed by us, couriered across India and abroad.

Drinking water
Sold at the ghats

Packaged drinking water under the Vedanz name, sold where the pilgrims actually are.

Ganga Jal goes to people who are not in Haridwar. Drinking water is sold to people who are.

Vedanz · Ganga Jal

How it works

  1. Order comes on the website, or goes along with an Online Pooja booking
  2. We fill and seal the bottle at Haridwar
  3. We pack it so it survives transport
  4. We courier it to the address
  5. For abroad, it travels with the pooja prasad in the same parcel

Why it fits us. Same devotee as Online Pooja. Same trust. Same parcel. We are not finding a new customer — we are serving the one we already have.

Vedanz · Drinking water

Three ways to do it

Way 1
Someone else's plant, our label
  • An existing plant makes the water
  • They print Vedanz on the bottle
  • They hold the licences, not us
  • Fastest to start
  • We learn if it sells before spending big
Way 2
Our own plant
  • We build our own bottling unit
  • BIS licence is compulsory — nobody can sell packaged water without it
  • FSSAI, trade licence, pollution clearance also ours
  • Both licence numbers must be printed on every bottle
  • Takes several months before the first bottle sells
  • Full control, best long term
Way 3
Pure trading
  • Buy someone else's brand, sell it at the ghats
  • No label, no licence of our own
  • Easiest — but we build nothing that is ours

Suggestion: start with Way 1. Vedanz bottles reach pilgrims quickly, we learn what sells, and we build the name. If it works, we build our own plant later — with real experience behind the decision instead of a guess.

ChaloKumbh · Service three

Doon Travellers Running

This one is not an idea. It is already working — real cabs, real drivers, real bookings, already linked with ChaloKumbh.

This is our proof. It shows we can actually run a service, not just plan one.

Doon Travellers

How it works

  1. Customer calls, WhatsApps, or books on the website
  2. We confirm the vehicle and the time
  3. Our partner operator sends the cab and driver
  4. Customer travels
  5. We handle the booking and the customer — the operator handles the vehicle

We do not buy vehicles. We partner with operators who already have them.

ChaloKumbh · Service four

USJ Stays

We do not own hotels. Hotels list their rooms with us, we bring them customers, and we take a share.

The same idea as OYO, Goibibo or Airbnb — but focused only on Kumbh regions.

Why we can do this. The big platforms charge hotels a lot, and small Haridwar hotels and dharamshalas are not happy about it. We can offer them a better deal because we are small and local.

We are not trying to beat MakeMyTrip across India. We only want to own Haridwar and the Kumbh regions.

USJ Stays

How it works

  1. We visit hotels, dharamshalas and guest houses in Haridwar
  2. They agree to list their rooms with us
  3. We put their rooms on our website with photos and details
  4. A pilgrim books through ChaloKumbh
  5. The hotel gets the guest, we take our share
  6. We handle the booking and the customer support
USJ Stays

What we need

  • Someone in Haridwar signing up hotels one by one. This is a feet-on-ground job, not a website job
  • Booking system on the website
  • Simple agreement paperwork with each hotel
  • Someone to handle bookings and customer calls

The timing point. During Ardh Kumbh, rooms in Haridwar will be almost impossible to find. If we tie up hotels before the rush, we will be holding rooms that everyone else needs.

And Kumbh comes back again. Whatever name we build now, we keep for the next one.

What all four have in common

  • Talking to the pandits
  • Filling and sending Ganga Jal
  • Signing up hotels

Without someone on the ground in Haridwar, none of these four move.
